The main points of how Plumbline works with a company's data. Full terms will replace this draft after legal review.

Status of the service

Plumbline is in development and not yet generally available. No SOC 2 report or other certification has been issued.

Your data

  • A portfolio company remains the controller of its employees' data; Plumbline processes it on the company's behalf.
  • Plumbline collects only the data described in the privacy policy and on Integrations and data use, and does not share it with any third party for that party's own use.
  • Connections are read-only except for the limited writes listed on the Security page. A company can revoke any connection at any time.
  • A sponsor sees a company's aggregates only while a data agreement between them is in force.

AI output and advice

AI output in Plumbline is a proposal until a named person approves it. The governance features organise evidence; they are not legal advice.

Retention and deletion

Retention periods and the export-and-close process are described in the privacy policy. Closing an account overrides the seven-year evidence retention period.
