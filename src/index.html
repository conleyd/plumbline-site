For private equity sponsors and their portfolio companies

The management system for AI-native transformation.

Private equity sponsors use Plumbline to see, steer and prove the AI-native progress of their portfolio companies. Each portfolio company uses it to measure itself, follow a prescribed roadmap, and show results in financial terms.

Agents collect, people confirm, every number shows its evidence.

In developmentPlumbline is not yet generally available. These pages describe how the system is designed to work, including exactly what it does with data from the tools a company connects.

What sponsors see

Scores, stages and aggregates across their portfolio companies, each traceable to the evidence behind it.

Sponsors see only aggregates at company and function level, and only while a data agreement with the portfolio company is in force. Individuals are never ranked, and nothing is shown for a group of fewer than five people.

What portfolio companies connect

The systems that show how work gets done, such as CRM, identity provider and card spend, through read-only connections.

Before signing in to any source, the company sees what Plumbline reads and never reads from it. Every source can be replaced by a file upload, and every connection can be revoked.

How data is protected

Each company's data is encrypted under its own data key and kept apart from every other company's in the database.

Connector credentials are decrypted only inside the task that uses them. Evidence is append-only and hash-chained. The content of AI prompts and chats is never collected. Read the security design.
