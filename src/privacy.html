Privacy policy

What Plumbline collects, who sees it, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no scripts, has no forms and uses no analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, as the host, receives the technical details any web request carries, such as IP address.

Who is responsible for the data

For the data of a portfolio company's employees, the portfolio company is the controller and Plumbline is the processor. For a sponsor's own users and the aggregates it receives, Plumbline is the sponsor's processor.

What data is collected

  • Account data: name, work email address and single sign-on identity for people who use Plumbline.
  • Data from connected sources: only what Integrations and data use lists for each source, and never what it lists as not read.
  • Directory data: people, managers, departments and start and end dates, used to match accounts to people.
  • Per-person AI usage: counts and metadata only, such as sessions, commits and cost. The content of AI prompts and chats is never collected.
  • Uploaded files and evidence that a company chooses to provide, such as financial packs and screenshots. Image metadata is removed on upload.
  • Audit records of sign-ins, approvals, exports, connector changes and every read of raw financials or one person's data.

Who sees it

  • Each person sees their own detail. A manager sees a person's detail only if that person chooses to share it, and the person can stop sharing at any time.
  • Sponsors see only company and function aggregates covering five or more people, and only while a data agreement with the portfolio company is in force. Sponsors never see raw financials or any individual's data.
  • No Plumbline staff member can view connector credentials. Support access to a company's data needs a grant the company's admin can see and revoke, and is logged.

Who it is shared with

None. Plumbline does not sell data or share it with any third party for that party's own use.

Deletion on request

  • A company can export its data and close its account at any time. Live data is deleted, the company's data keys are destroyed so that encrypted evidence cannot be read, backups expire by day 28, and a deletion certificate is issued on day 30. The certificate lists what is kept: scores a selling sponsor has already seen, anonymised benchmark contributions, and the record of the closure itself.
  • An employee of a portfolio company can ask for a copy of their data or for it to be erased. Requests go through the company's admin, because the company is the controller.
