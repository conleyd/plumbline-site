How a portfolio company gets started

Onboarding has three parts: accept the invitation, connect the company's tools, and see where AI adoption stands across the company, with the evidence for each reading.

1. Invite

Each contact at the portfolio company receives an email invitation. The link works once, is bound to the invited email address, and expires after 72 hours.

People can also sign in with their company's single sign-on through Microsoft Entra ID or Google. A company can require single sign-on for all of its members.

A sponsor sees nothing from a portfolio company until that company accepts a data agreement. The agreement sets what aggregates the sponsor may read, and accepting or changing it is recorded in the company's audit log.

2. Connect tools

Every source is connected through the same five steps, shown in a side panel. The panel is generated from the same registry entry the connector code runs from, so what it says a connector reads is what the connector reads.

  1. Why connect. Which checklist items the source can verify and which measures it feeds.
  2. Sign in or add key. An OAuth sign-in, an app install, or an API key that a person at the company pastes in. Plumbline's agents never handle passwords.
  3. Review what we read and never read. The data read, the data never read, and each permission requested with the reason for it. If the vendor grants narrower permissions than requested, the panel shows which are missing.
  4. Map and match. How accounts in the source are matched to people, and any mapping the company sets once, such as which roles carry a sales quota.
  5. Test and preview. One bounded read, with at most 20 sample rows shown and not stored.

After the first successful test, Plumbline reads the last 90 days of history, newest first, so results can start before the backfill finishes. For Salesforce it reads all open opportunities and those closed in the last twelve months, because revenue measures need the whole book.

A connection can be revoked at any time. Revoking calls the vendor's revoke endpoint where one exists, deletes the stored credential, and stops queued work. History already collected stays, with its evidence.

The exact permissions for each source are on Integrations and data use.

3. See the AI-adoption heatmap

The heatmap shows where AI adoption stands across the company's functions. Each cell carries how well its reading is supported, not only the reading itself.

Attested
A named person at the company confirmed it.
Verified
A connected source shows it. Connecting a source moves its cells from Attested to Verified.
Stale
The source is more than one cycle late or the connection is no longer live. The last good value stays on screen with its date and a lower confidence.
Not connected
No value has ever arrived. The cell says so and links to the connect panel. It never shows zero.

Opening any cell shows where its number came from. Suggestions made by AI stay proposals until a named person approves them. A sponsor sees the same picture only as company and function aggregates covering five or more people.
